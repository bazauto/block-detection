[CC-BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) with this exception:

### Project symbols derived from KiCad library symbols

`bazauto-block-detection.kicad_sym` is generated by `scripts/gen_schematic.py`. It copies two stock symbols, renames
them, and changes their properties:
